START HERE
==========

To open the control dashboard, run:

    bash Start/start-dashboard.sh

(or double-click start-dashboard.sh and choose "Run in Terminal").

Your browser opens http://localhost:8088
Login:  admin   /   the password in  admin/admin_password.txt

From the dashboard you can:
  - See if the Bot and WhatsApp are connected (green = good).
  - Start / Stop the bot.
  - If WhatsApp labels are NOT linked, press "Link WhatsApp" and scan
    the QR code with your phone (WhatsApp Business > Linked devices).
  - See and manage all customers and their labels.
